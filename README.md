# Hush

## Description
This is Hush! A web app for removing background audio from video and enhancing voices.

## Why?
I found myself in the all-too-familiar position of having taken video of an important event, to have the audio be corrupted by wind. But it's not just wind that corrupts the audio of important life events like weddings, graduations, and vacations. It's also loud AC units, background voices, and car traffic. I was tired of having unclear audio when playing back videos I took to capture important memories, so I made Hush. With Hush people don't need programming knowledge to use a state of the art open source AI that removes this annoying background audio.

## 🚀 Quick Start
Navigate to [hush.andrewvincent.dev](https://hush.andrewvincent.dev/) create an account with Google and upload a video file!
When it's done you will be able to view your video on the site through your account.
